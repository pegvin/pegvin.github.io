There is a fundamental problem to existence.
To understand it, you must ask yourself that
how does something just _exist_?

Phrasing it differently, how do the things
that make something exist, exist themselves? And
obviously things that make something exist must
also need something to exist themselves, So what
made that exist?

As mankind advanced in science, we started looking
into how our world came to be. The most widely accepted
theory is that it all began with a _big bang_.

But how? Our species won't live long enough to find
answers to it & I doubt any specie has the comprehension
to understand it.

> The Universe is under no obligation to make sense to you.
> ~ Neil deGrasse Tyson
