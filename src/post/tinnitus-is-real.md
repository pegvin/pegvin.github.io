Tinnitus is often explained as "ringing in the ears", In reality
it can differ alot. From Roaring to Humming. For me it's a constant
_tiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii_.

It's not loud. Hardly noticeable in my day-to-day activities, but
what bothers me is the fact that how young I am to have this issue.

Tinnitus can be caused by alot of things but the most common amongst
young people is prolonged exposure to loud sounds.

For me it was a combination of:
1. Loud music (often via earphones).
2. Loud wind noise caused by motorcycling.

The 1st point reflects my stupidity, But the second point reflects
my obliviousness.

When riding motorcycles, The biggest source of noise is wind. Even
the good helmets are not enough to reduce the wind noise to a safe
level. Combine this with an hour of commute daily, results in
_tiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii_ in your ears.

The prevention is simple. Ear plugs. Do your research, It's very
subjective but the best noise reduction you will get is from
those orange foam ear plugs.

It's worth it to invest in such a product than to be potentially
dependent on an hearing aid for rest of your life. And trust me,
If you unfortunately get Tinnitus & Become aware of it. The sound
will never leave you.
